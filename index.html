The story

Step into timeless elegance

Some places don't just welcome you. They make time stand still.

Built in the early 1900s, Villa Roccolo was lovingly restored with deep respect for the family who first called it home. Original portraits still watch over the rooms, antique furnishings carry a century of stories, and every corner holds a quiet charm, now paired with modern comfort.

From the moment you arrive, something shifts: the light on the lake, the stillness of the garden. Six bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms give family and friends space to gather, and to slip away for a book by the pool, a long lunch on the terrace, or a swim in the lake.

Outside

Palms, pool and the whole lake

The villa sits in its lush garden on the hillside. Every corner looks out over the water, from the pool to the balcony where dinner is served.

The house

Rooms with a memory

Elegant living spaces with antique furnishings and authentic period details throughout: a living room with a fireplace and a kitchen big enough for everyone to cook together.

Six bedrooms

Sleep under crystal

Each bedroom tells its own story, blending historic charm with modern comfort for a truly restorative stay. Carved walnut beds, white linen, tall windows, balconies, and a chandelier in almost every room. Click a room to see its photos.

A day at Roccolo

From first light to the last candle

Nothing here needs planning. The day arranges itself around the lake.

7:00

The sun comes up over the water

The house faces east across the lake. Early risers get to enjoy the spectacle.

9:00

Breakfast, slowly

A big farmhouse table in the kitchen, or coffee carried out through the French doors to the balcony.

12:00

The pool

Forty square metres of water on the edge of the hillside, with loungers and the lake between you and the mountains.

15:00

Down to the lake

Ten minutes on foot through the garden brings you to a small pebble beach and clear, cool water. The climb back is a good little workout.

20:00

Dinner for ten on the balcony

The stone table seats everyone. The clouds over the far shore do the rest.

23:00

The far shore lights up

One lantern, a quiet lake, and the villages opposite strung out like a necklace.

Outdoors

Pool
Unheated, about 40 m², with two small gates that can be closed.
Garden
Fully fenced, with panoramic views and a ping-pong table under the large pine tree.
Lake
A lovely walk down to a small pebble beach. The way back up is a good little workout.
Road
The access road is gravel: fine for large cars, not for very low ones.

Indoors

Cooling
No air conditioning. The thick old walls keep the house cool, and every bedroom has a fan.
Wi-Fi
Throughout the house, though not at the pool.
Kitchen
Fully equipped. The living room has a TV and a fireplace, and there's also an office.
Laundry
Washer, dryer and drying rack. Extra cleaning during your stay on request.

Children

Best for babies not yet crawling, or children of 8 and over who can swim. The terraces and pool make the house unsuitable for toddlers.

Dogs

One dog is welcome. The garden is fenced, and there is a €60 extra cleaning fee.
